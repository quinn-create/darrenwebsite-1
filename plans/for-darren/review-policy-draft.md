# Draft: how the website shows Google reviews (for Darren to approve)

**Why this exists.** Darren asked for his live Google star rating on the website (decision D6). A rating on the firm's website is part of the firm's advertising, so the plan asks for a short written policy first (conflict C4). This is a draft for Darren to edit or approve. It isn't legal advice, and Darren decides whether it meets his professional obligations. The optional ethics review (D12) is available if he wants a second opinion.

Nothing about reviews is on the site until Darren approves this and the items at the end exist.

## The policy (draft)

1. **What's shown.** Only the average star rating and the total number of reviews from Darren's Google Business Profile, with a link to read every review on Google. No review text, quotes, names or photos are shown on the website.
2. **Shown as-is.** The numbers come straight from Google and update automatically. The firm never edits, rounds up, filters or chooses which reviews count.
3. **No incentives.** The firm doesn't offer anything (discounts, gifts or favours) in exchange for a review, and doesn't write or ask others to write reviews.
4. **Asking for reviews.** If the firm asks clients for reviews, it asks every client the same way, after the matter ends, and makes clear a review is optional.
5. **Wording beside the rating.** The rating always appears with this line (Darren to approve the exact words):
   > "Ratings are from Google reviews by individual clients. Every case is different, and past client experiences don't predict the outcome of any other matter."
6. **Where it appears.** At most one small rating line per page, never in the page's main headline and never beside a description of case results. It doesn't appear on the Contact page.
7. **If the rating becomes unavailable** (the Google profile is suspended or the connection fails), the line simply disappears; nothing is shown in its place.
8. **Record-keeping.** Screenshots of the rating as shown are kept with the firm's 2-year advertising archive, like every other page.
9. **Review.** Darren reviews this policy once a year, or sooner if the rules change.
