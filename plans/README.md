# Plans

- `signal-website-plan.md`: the build and launch plan for the full Darren Drake website in the Signal 1A style (24 September 2026). Start with "Plan at a glance".
- `research/`: the six research reports the plan is based on:
  - `current-site.md`: read-only audit of ddrakelaw.com (pages, content, contact form, technical notes, old addresses to redirect).
  - `codebase.md`: gap audit of the `site/` demo, and how the new photo is used.
  - `design.md`: page-by-page design spec extending Signal 1A.
  - `legal.md`: Tennessee advertising, privacy and accessibility checklist, with sources and VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED labels. It is not legal advice.
  - `intake-hosting.md`: intake destination options, hosting, domain cutover and security.
  - `seo.md`: search, local listings, structured data and measurement.
- `images/`: preview of the new photo in the 1A concept, and the 4:5 hero crop.
